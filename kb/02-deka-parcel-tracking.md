# Deka Post — Parcel Acceptance, Exchange & Tracking

From 2023 to 2025, Samira worked as a frontend developer at Deka Post (Entekhab Electronic). This system manages parcel acceptance, exchange, tracking and distribution across the postal network. Postal clerks use it all day.

## The problem

Parcels move between agencies and postal branches. When the exchange screen was unclear, clerks made mistakes and parcels went to the wrong place.

## What Samira did

- Rebuilt the parcel exchange screens and logic between agencies, so transfers across branches are clear and hard to get wrong.
- Built the frontend with React, Redux, MUI, React Query and React Hook Form.
- Coordinated work across the fourteen-person development team and helped define clear goals for each milestone.

## Results

These are team results, measured and reported by the Deka Post team:

- 20% fewer human errors in parcel exchange on Deka's parcel tracking system.
- 30% faster parcel processing on Deka's parcel tracking system.
- On a team of fourteen, the team reached major milestones 10% ahead of schedule.
