# 3click — Sales & Support Management Panel

A second project at Deltaban Group: the panel the 3click team uses to manage sales and customer support in the travel business.

## What Samira did

- Built the panel with Vue.js and AG Grid for fast, interactive data tables.
- Improved the speed and usability of the screens the sales and support team use every day.

## Results

- 10% more sales after a speed and usability pass on the 3click support panel, measured and reported by the 3click team.
