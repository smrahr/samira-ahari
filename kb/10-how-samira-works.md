# How Samira Works

Four values, each one a behaviour.

## 1. Built for the operator, not the demo

The person who uses the screen for eight hours decides what "good" means. Their mistakes and workarounds are the brief. Samira asks to watch real users before she designs, even though it makes the start slower.

## 2. Fast under real load

She tests with real data volumes, not ten sample rows. Virtualized lists, server-side grids, lazy loading — whatever keeps the screen quick with a million records.

## 3. Code the next developer can inherit

Reusable components, typed APIs, generated clients and a clear structure. The project should work well without her. She is happy to make herself easy to replace.

## 4. On schedule, in writing

Plans and decisions are written down, so a team can act without a call. She reports milestones early, good news or bad.
