# Netpeaksell — B2B Group Ordering Platform

In 2023, at Zhikan Co., Samira worked on Netpeaksell, a B2B platform for group ordering in the pharmaceutical industry (netpeaksell.ir).

## What Samira did

- Fixed the filtering bugs that showed wrong results to buyers.
- Built and improved features with Next.js 13 and Tailwind CSS, plus Day.js, Axios and a date picker.
- Worked with a backend developer on the API integration.

## Results

- 20% better platform performance on Netpeaksell, measured and reported by the Netpeaksell team.
