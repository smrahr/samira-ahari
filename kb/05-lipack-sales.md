# Lipack — Visitor & Sales Management Panel

Also at Zhikan Co. in 2023. Lipack's internal panel lets managers track sales visitors, sales activities, assigned tasks and goals.

## What Samira did

- Designed and built the internal panel.
- Built the frontend with React, Redux Toolkit, Formik and ApexCharts.
- Tuned performance so the charts and tables stay smooth with very large data sets.

## Results

- 1 million data points handled smoothly in one panel on Lipack Sales, as reported by the Lipack team.
