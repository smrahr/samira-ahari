# 3click — Travel Booking Website

From 2022 to 2023, at Deltaban Group, Samira worked on 3click, a travel booking platform (3click.com).

## What Samira did

- Built booking features with Next.js, TypeScript and the Context API, with Strapi as the CMS and Figma for design hand-off.
- Used list virtualization (react-virtualized) and lazy loading so long result lists stay fast.
- Coordinated tasks and workflows for a cross-functional team, using Jira.

## Results

- 30% faster website on the 3click travel booking platform, measured and reported by the 3click team.
