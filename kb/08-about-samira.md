# About Samira Ahari

Samira Ahari is a frontend developer for admin panels, dashboards and data-heavy web apps. She works with React, Next.js and TypeScript. She is open for contracts.

## Short bio

Samira builds the software teams use all day — admin panels, back-offices and data-heavy web apps — and makes it fast, clear and hard to get wrong. She has built these systems for banking, postal, pricing, sales and travel teams.

## Experience

- 5+ years of frontend work, since 2018.
- 7 production systems shipped across 5 companies.
- 2025–present: Nova Lab — banking administration platform.
- 2023–2025: Deka Post (Entekhab Electronic) — parcel tracking system, and a regional pricing and contracts panel.
- 2023: Zhikan Co. — Netpeaksell B2B ordering platform, and Lipack's sales management panel.
- 2022–2023: Deltaban Group — 3click travel booking website, and 3click's sales and support panel.
- 2018–2022: Padideh Fekre Mehtar — her first role. She built custom websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and did logo and branding design.

## Education and certificates

- Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Mazandaran University of Science and Technology, Iran.
- Front End Development Libraries — freeCodeCamp.
- Meta Front-End Developer — Coursera.

## Tagline

"Code that thinks." Here "thinks" means code that has thought about the user before the user arrives: the form that stops the wrong entry, the grid that stays fast with a million rows, the error state that tells an operator what to do next.
